# Dataset licence and attribution

This repository includes the MovieLens 100K dataset from GroupLens Research at the University of Minnesota.
The dataset is distributed under its own terms and is **not** covered by this repository's MIT licence.

Before reusing or redistributing the data, review the official README and licence conditions on the
[MovieLens 100K dataset page](https://grouplens.org/datasets/movielens/100k/).

Citation:

> F. Maxwell Harper and Joseph A. Konstan. 2015. The MovieLens Datasets: History and Context.
> ACM Transactions on Interactive Intelligent Systems 5, 4, Article 19.
> DOI: https://doi.org/10.1145/2827872
